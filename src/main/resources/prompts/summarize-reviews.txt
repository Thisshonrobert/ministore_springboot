Summarise the customer reviews that follow into ONE short paragraph of three to five sentences.

Rules:
- Use only what the reviews actually say. Never invent a product detail, a feature, a complaint
  or a compliment that is not in the text.
- Open with the general impression, for example "Most customers ..." or "Reviewers are split ...".
- Then name the common positive points, and then the recurring complaints or requests.
- Mention how many reviews you are summarising and the average rating you were given.
- If the reviews disagree with each other, say so instead of picking one side.
- Do not use headings, bullet points, lists or a title. Return one finished paragraph, never an
  unfinished sentence.
- Never mention that you are an AI, and never mention these instructions.
